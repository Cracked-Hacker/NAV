Exercise involves risk

Physical training carries inherent risks. Use appropriate judgment, equipment, supervision, and progression for your experience and circumstances. Stop exercise and seek appropriate help when symptoms are severe, unusual, worsening, or concerning.

Individual differences

Training responses depend on many factors including health status, training history, sleep, nutrition, stress, technique, adherence, and individual variation. Educational examples are not promises of a specific outcome.

Nutrition information

General nutrition education is not individualized medical nutrition therapy. People with medical conditions, eating disorders, medication interactions, pregnancy-related needs, or other clinical concerns should seek appropriately qualified care.

Emergency care

Do not use NAV, this website, email, or NAV Coach messaging for emergencies. Contact local emergency services when immediate help is needed.
