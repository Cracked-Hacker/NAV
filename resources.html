NAV FAQ

Straight answers before you click anywhere else.

No invented pricing, customer counts, certifications, or feature-completeness claims.

What is NAV?

NAV combines human fitness coaching, a focused coaching app, and practical exercise-science education. The public site explains and teaches; the authenticated NAV Coach app handles private coaching work.

Does NAV replace a coach?

No. NAV is designed to reduce fragmented tools and administrative friction while keeping the coach responsible for interpretation, programming, communication, and adjustment.

Who is NAV Coach for?

NAV Coach is being built for coaches who want a clearer operating system and athletes who want a simpler place to see, perform, record, and understand their training.

Can coaches bring existing spreadsheet programs into NAV?

Status: In development. NAV is actively developing a switching path for structured spreadsheet programming. It is not presented as a currently released production feature, so coaches should not rely on it as available today.

Why does NAV separate prescription from performance?

Because “the coach asked for it” and “the athlete completed it” are different facts. Preserving both makes history and progress interpretation more trustworthy.

How does NAV treat estimated strength?

Calculated strength values are estimates, not measured one-repetition maximums. NAV’s product rules favor conservative use of estimates and clear labels rather than presenting calculated values as certainty.

What are RPE and RIR?

RPE describes perceived effort; RIR describes estimated repetitions remaining. They can help communicate training difficulty when used consistently. Learn RPE & RIR →

What happens to private client data?

Private coaching information belongs in the authenticated NAV Coach app, not on the public website. Public pages should never publish real client workouts, messages, nutrition records, schedules, or health information.

Is NAV medical care?

No. NAV provides fitness coaching and exercise education. It does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure medical conditions. Concerning symptoms, injuries, or health questions belong with an appropriately licensed healthcare professional.
