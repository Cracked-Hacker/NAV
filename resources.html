NAV FAQ

Questions about NAV

Available services and app features may change; contact NAV for current coaching options and fees.

What is NAV?

NAV combines human fitness coaching, a focused coaching app, and practical exercise-science education. The public site explains and teaches; the authenticated NAV Coach app handles private coaching work.

Does NAV replace a coach?

No. NAV helps organize the work around coaching. The coach still makes the training decisions and communicates with the athlete.

Who is NAV Coach for?

NAV Coach is the private training app for coaches and athletes. Coaches work with programs and athlete context; athletes access assigned sessions and log their work.

How do I access NAV beta features?

Private beta access is not available yet. Creating an account won't unlock unfinished courses or materials. Contact NAV if you're interested in testing.

Private testing status →

Why does NAV separate prescription from performance?

An assigned workout isn't proof it was completed. NAV keeps the prescription separate from the sets an athlete logs.

How does NAV treat estimated strength?

Calculated strength values are estimates, not measured one-repetition maximums. NAV’s product rules favor conservative use of estimates and clear labels rather than presenting calculated values as certainty.

What are RPE and RIR?

RPE describes perceived effort; RIR describes estimated repetitions remaining. They can help communicate training difficulty when used consistently. Read NAV science standards →

What happens to private client data?

This public website does not display client workout logs, messages, nutrition records, schedules, or health information. Those records belong in the signed-in NAV Coach app.

Is NAV medical care?

No. NAV provides fitness coaching and exercise education. It does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure medical conditions. Concerning symptoms, injuries, or health questions belong with an appropriately licensed healthcare professional.
