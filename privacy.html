Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: October 29, 2026.

NAV’s public website is designed to collect as little information as practical. This initial public release does not include advertising trackers, behavioral profiling, or marketing analytics.

Public-site data

Basic hosting infrastructure may process technical request data needed to serve pages securely and reliably. NAV does not intentionally place private NAV Coach records into public-site analytics or marketing systems.

Forms and email

If you choose to use a linked application form or email NAV, the information you submit is handled for the purpose of responding to your request. Avoid sending urgent or highly sensitive medical information through ordinary email.

Your NAV account and My NAV

The website and NAV Coach use the same NAV account and immutable account identifier. When you log in, My NAV retrieves your authorized profile, account capabilities, coaching connections, subscription records, and invoices from NAV’s existing Supabase system. Public marketing pages do not display these private records. NAV does not store your password itself.

The website stores its own authentication session in your browser so you can remain logged in. Other NAV products can maintain separate sessions for the same account. Log out when using a shared device. Account and security changes use the existing NAV authentication system; email changes require your current password and email confirmation.

Classroom study progress currently remains on the device where you study; it is not treated as an account-synced completion or certification record. The NAV account privacy policy and health-data consent describe additional product handling.

External links

External services such as Google Forms, Instagram, YouTube, or the NAV Coach hosting platform may process data under their own privacy policies.

Future analytics and payments

If NAV later enables analytics or payments, this policy should be updated before those systems collect data, with privacy-conscious defaults and clear separation from private coaching information.
