NAV Coaching

Coaching built around the person doing the training.

NAV coaching uses clear goals, repeatable training, feedback, and progress data to make practical decisions—not to chase novelty for its own sake.

Programming

A plan with a reason.

Exercise selection, loading, sets, reps, effort, progression, and scheduling should serve a defined training goal and fit the person performing it.

Feedback

Observe what actually happened.

Logged training, technique, recovery context, and communication help the plan respond to real outcomes rather than guesses.

Education

Understand the decisions.

Coaching should make you more capable of understanding your own training, not more dependent on unexplained instructions.

Who it can fit
