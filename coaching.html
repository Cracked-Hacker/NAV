Before You Apply Online

What to Think About

Tell me what you want to accomplish, your current training experience, and any limitations involving equipment, scheduling, or training. We’ll discuss the plan before getting started.

After You Apply

I’ll review your application and reach out to discuss your goals, schedule, and whether we’re a good fit. Before we begin, we’ll confirm pricing, start date, payment arrangements, and what you want out of your coaching experience.
