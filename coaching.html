NAV Coaching

Coaching built around the person doing the training.

NAV coaching uses clear goals, repeatable training, feedback, and progress data to make practical decisions—not to chase novelty or hand responsibility to an algorithm.

Ask About CoachingSee how NAV Coach supports it

Programming

A plan with a reason.

Exercise selection, loading, sets, reps, effort, progression, and scheduling should serve a defined training goal and fit the person performing it.

Feedback

Observe what actually happened.

Logged training, technique, recovery context, and communication help the plan respond to real outcomes rather than guesses.

Education

Understand the decisions.

Coaching should make you more capable of understanding your own training, not more dependent on unexplained instructions.

The relationship

Coach leads the process. Athlete supplies the real-world signal.

NAV Coach is the workspace around that relationship. It can organize the plan and history; it does not replace communication or judgment.

What the coach is responsible for

  • 01Understand goals, history, constraints, and available equipment.
  • 02Build and adjust a training plan with a clear rationale.
  • 03Review performance and useful feedback rather than assuming completion.
  • 04Communicate scope and refer out when a question belongs with licensed healthcare.

What the athlete brings

  • 01Honest training logs and feedback about what actually happened.
  • 02Communication when schedule, preferences, equipment, or recovery context changes.
  • 03Consistent effort toward the agreed plan rather than chasing daily novelty.
  • 04Appropriate medical care for symptoms or conditions outside fitness coaching scope.

Who it can fit

Strength, muscle, performance, and general fitness.

Exact services and availability may change. NAV only publishes offerings that are actually available and does not promise guaranteed outcomes.

Good fit when you want

  • OKA structured training plan with clear priorities.
  • OKProgression that can respond to performance and recovery.
  • OKTechnique and training feedback from a real coach.
  • OKA mobile system for training, history, progress, and coaching context.

Not a replacement for

  • —Medical diagnosis, treatment, or rehabilitation.
  • —Emergency care or management of concerning symptoms.
  • —Licensed dietetics or individualized medical nutrition therapy.
  • —A guarantee of a particular body-composition or performance result.

How to begin

Ask → talk → decide → onboard.

Start with the coaching application. If it looks like a reasonable fit, the next step is a conversation about goals, history, schedule, expectations, scope, and the appropriate coaching setup.
