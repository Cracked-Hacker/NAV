NAV Coach · human coach in control

A training workspace for coaches and athletes.

Coaches assign training. Athletes open sessions, log completed sets, and review progress. NAV Coach keeps those activities together in one authenticated app.

Open NAV CoachSign in or create an account

For athletes

Know what to do. Record what happened. See what is changing.

The daily workflow begins with the assigned session and ends with a saved record of the work performed.

01 · Train

Follow the assigned work.

Open the training workspace, see the current plan, and use the app during the session.

02 · Log

Record performance as performance.

What the coach prescribed and what the athlete actually completed remain different facts.

03 · Progress

Use history to add context.

Training history and progress views help turn past work into better future coaching decisions.

For coaches

Keep the person, the plan, and the follow-up connected.

A coach needs the client’s plan, completed work, and relevant feedback in context before making the next programming decision.

01 · Assign

Program the next session.

Set exercises and intended reps, loading, and effort without marking that work completed.

02 · Review

See what was actually logged.

Compare the prescription with saved sets and athlete feedback. Unknown ≠ zero.

03 · Adjust

Change the next plan.

Use recorded training and your coaching judgment, not a fabricated readiness score.
