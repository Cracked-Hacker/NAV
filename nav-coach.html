How the training workflow fits together

The plan is not the performance record.

A coach's intended work and an athlete's completed work are different information. Keep them distinguishable when reviewing progress.

01 · Assign

The coach plans.

Exercises, reps, loading, and intended effort describe what to do.

02 · Record

The athlete logs.

Completed sets describe what happened, which can differ from the original plan.

03 · Review

The coach decides.

Use actual training history and context rather than treating missing entries as zero.

Private coaching information, including training records and client messages, belongs in the authenticated NAV Coach app—not on public website pages. An account alone does not create a coaching relationship or grant additional permissions.
