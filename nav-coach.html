NAV Coach · human coach in control

A simpler place to coach—and to be coached.

NAV Coach is the private training workspace behind NAV. It keeps the plan, the athlete’s actual work, progress, and coaching context connected so software supports the relationship instead of replacing it.

Open NAV CoachExplore human coaching

For athletes

Know what to do. Record what happened. See what is changing.

The athlete experience is built around the work that matters during real training—not a wall of dashboards.

01 · Train

Follow the assigned work.

Open the training workspace, see the current plan, and use the app during the session.

02 · Log

Record performance as performance.

What the coach prescribed and what the athlete actually completed remain different facts.

03 · Progress

Use history to add context.

Training history and progress views help turn past work into better future coaching decisions.

For coaches

Keep the person, the plan, and the follow-up connected.

NAV is being built as a coaching operating system: fewer disconnected tools, clearer client context, and less administrative friction around the actual work of coaching.

A

Coach judgment stays central.

NAV can organize information and reduce repetitive work. The coach still interprets context and decides what to prescribe next.

B

Prescription ≠ performance.

A planned set is not silently treated as a completed set. Keeping those facts separate makes history more trustworthy.

C

Unknown ≠ zero.

Missing information should stay missing rather than becoming a made-up value that looks precise.

D

Calculated values stay labeled.

Estimated strength and similar derived values are useful context, not direct measurements or guarantees.

Existing coach workflows

Your spreadsheet history should not become a rebuilding tax.

NAV’s direction is to let coaches bring structured programming from spreadsheets into the coaching workflow instead of manually recreating years of work.

Status: In development. Spreadsheet import is not presented here as a currently released production feature.

The website explains NAV. NAV Coach holds private coaching work.
