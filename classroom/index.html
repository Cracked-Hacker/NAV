What counts as good Classroom content

Useful enough for the gym. Serious enough to check.

NAV separates established evidence from interpretation and practical coaching heuristics. References are shown so learners and professional reviewers can inspect the source trail.

Evidence

No fitness folklore presented as fact.

Prioritize consensus guidance, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, peer-reviewed research, authoritative textbooks, and recognized professional organizations.

Application

Every module should change a decision.

Learning objectives, checks, and solo labs are designed around what the learner can explain or do—not just what they can remember.

Scope

Education, not individualized medical care.

Classroom material explains exercise and training concepts. It does not diagnose injuries, prescribe treatment, or replace appropriate medical care.
