Train smarter. Know why.

The practical foundations of training science first: short lessons, plain language, quick checks, solo gym labs, and source lists that make claims inspectable.

One idea to remember

Training is an applied experiment.

Train → observe → recover → evaluate → adjust. Apply a stimulus, see how you perform and recover, then change the plan when the evidence from your training supports it.

Evidence standard

No fitness folklore presented as fact.

Prefer consensus guidance, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, peer-reviewed research, and recognized professional references. Label uncertainty when evidence is mixed or limited.

Scope

Education, not individualized medical care.

Classroom material explains exercise and training concepts. It does not diagnose injuries, prescribe treatment, or replace appropriate medical care.
