Starter Kit · Module 2

Building Muscle

What actually makes muscles grow? Useful training volume, sufficient effort, exercise selection, range of motion, nutrition, and recovery all contribute to muscle growth.

Key takeawayHypertrophy is produced by a training system, not one magic rep range or exercise.

Lesson 1

Many loads can build muscle

Muscle growth can occur across a broad range of resistance-training loads when sets are performed with sufficient effort. Heavier and lighter loading are not identical, but hypertrophy is not restricted to one narrow repetition range.

Lesson 2

Volume matters, but more is not automatically better

Across groups, performing multiple challenging sets and accumulating more weekly training volume can support greater hypertrophy than very low volumes. Individual recovery and diminishing returns still matter, so volume should be increased deliberately rather than endlessly.

Lesson 3

Exercise execution changes the stimulus

Exercise choice, usable range of motion, stability, and technique affect which muscles are challenged and how consistently you can progress. A theoretically good exercise is only useful if it fits the goal and the person performing it.

Quick check

Can you apply the idea?

Which is the best evidence-based summary of hypertrophy training?
Reveal answer

Best answer: Several loading ranges can build muscle when training is sufficiently challenging and enough useful work is performed.

Evidence supports hypertrophy across a range of loads. The broader program—including effort, volume, exercise selection, nutrition, and recovery—matters.

Solo gym lab

Compare two exercises for one muscle

Pick one muscle you want to develop and two exercises available in your gym.

  1. Perform a controlled working set of each exercise with a safe submaximal load.
  2. Compare stability, usable range of motion, comfort, and how clearly the target muscle is challenged.
  3. Record which exercise is easier to standardize and progress over time.
  4. Choose which one better fits your current goal and write one sentence explaining why.
