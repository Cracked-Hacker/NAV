Your learning record

Progress

Completion, quiz status, and mastery checks are stored only on this device. NAV treats mastery as different from simply opening or finishing a page.

Completion

Did you work through it?

Completion records that you finished or intentionally marked the quiz, lab, or assignment done. It does not prove mastery.

Mastery

Can you retrieve and apply it later?

Mastery is a separate state. A week must have its quiz, all labs, and assignment complete plus a successful mastery retrieval check before it becomes Mastered.

Mastery map

New → Learning → Familiar → Proficient → Mastered

These states make progress more meaningful than page completion alone. Mastery should still be revisited through spaced retrieval rather than treated as permanent.
