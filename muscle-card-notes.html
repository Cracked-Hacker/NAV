Muscle card study notes

Use these notes alongside the original 379 cards. Each number is the card’s position in the download.

045 · Serratus anterior

For a standard anatomy question about innervation, give the long thoracic nerve and the usual root contributions of C5, C6, and C7. The range printed on the card extends one root beyond that usual pattern.

Read the upper-limb anatomy reference.

050 · Subclavius

The headings on this action card are misplaced. To recall attachments, trace the muscle from the first rib to the underside of the middle clavicle. To recall its action, picture the clavicle being steadied and drawn downward. Its nerve supply is the nerve to subclavius, usually C5 and C6.

Self-check. The entries labeled “NS” on card 050 are muscles listed as partners in movement. Which nerve would you write in that field? Compare your answer with card 378.

See the subclavius attachments and nerve.

301 · Gastrocnemius

Recall the two joint actions first. Gastrocnemius can plantarflex the ankle and flex the knee. It contains both slow- and fast-twitch fibers. In a study of 32 people, soleus had a larger proportion of slow-twitch fibers than gastrocnemius. That comparison cannot establish one person’s fiber proportions or sprint ability.

Read the human muscle fiber study by Edgerton, Smith, and Simpson.
