About NAV

Coaching, software, and science education.

I want people to understand their training, not just follow instructions. NAV brings together my coaching, the NAV Coach app, and science-based study resources. Classroom courses and certifications are still in development.

Founder · Coach Nate

Meet Coach Nate

I graduated from Western Washington University in 2023 with a B.S. in Kinesiology (pre-healthcare specialization), a B.A. in Biology, and a minor in Chemistry. I’ve worked in coaching, patient care, laboratory research, and anatomy teaching, and I coauthored a peer-reviewed paper.

I’m a NASM Certified Personal Trainer (CPT), certified May 29, 2025 through May 29, 2027.

Why NAV exists

Why I started NAV.

I’m building NAV Coach to keep prescribed workouts, completed sets, and coach-client communication together. My study resources explain the science behind training.

My background

Professional experience.

Hours are estimates from my November 2026 résumé.

Coaching and athletics

I’m a personal trainer, strength coach, and group fitness instructor at Blinnergy in Woodinville (since June 2025), and a fitness trainer with EXOS at Google's Seattle South Lake Union campus (since March 2026). My résumé estimates 1,300+ coaching hours at Blinnergy and 700+ hours at EXOS.

I’ve coached youth lacrosse and volunteered in strength sports. I’ve also competed in powerlifting.

Patient care and rehabilitation settings

I worked as a nursing assistant at North Cascades Health and Rehabilitation and through Staffers/Avalon Healthcare (more than 1,600 hours combined). I also spent more than 800 hours as a clinical aide at FYZICAL Therapy & Balance Centers.

Scientific research and publication

I spent more than 300 hours in Dr. Catherine Clark’s environmental chemistry lab at WWU, using microscopy, DAPI staining, spectroscopy, and quantitative image analysis. I co-presented our work on ethanol and acetaldehyde cycling in estuarine waters at an American Chemical Society conference in March 2023.

Later, as a University of Washington School of Social Work research assistant with Dr. Angelique Day, I reviewed literature and helped develop a manuscript. I’m a coauthor of a 2024 journal article on Medicaid contraceptive-care claims among youth in foster care. View the publication by DOI →

Teaching and community science

I was an undergraduate teaching assistant for WWU’s BIOL 348 Anatomy & Physiology lab in 2022 (more than 60 hours). I also helped develop community biotechnology lessons at Sound Bio Labs and shadowed researchers in the University of Washington Yang Lab.
