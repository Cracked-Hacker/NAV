About NAV

A training company built by a coach.

NAV provides personal coaching and a private training app; education and experimental products remain available only to invited beta testers. Founded by Nate Holochwost, the company is developing practical ways to program training, record performance, and teach the reasoning behind coaching decisions.

Founder · Coach Nate

Science background. Coaching practice. Clear scope.

Nate Holochwost holds a B.S. in Kinesiology, a B.A. in Biology, and a Chemistry minor from Western Washington University. His background also includes fitness coaching, patient-care experience, teaching, and published research.

Why NAV exists

Good coaching should not require a pile of disconnected systems.

Coaches often have to split programming, athlete logs, communication, progress, education, and historical context across different tools. NAV’s product direction is to connect those jobs while preserving the flexibility and human judgment that made the original coaching workflow useful.
