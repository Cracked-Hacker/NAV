About NAV

Better tools for human coaching.

NAV is a human-coach-led coaching and education company building a focused operating system around the work coaches and athletes already do: plan, train, observe, communicate, learn, and adjust.

Founder · Coach Nate

Science background. Coaching practice. Clear scope.

Nate Holochwost’s academic background includes biology and kinesiology with chemistry coursework, alongside fitness coaching, personal training certification, and research experience.

NAV uses that background to communicate exercise science carefully—not to imply physician, physical-therapy, dietetics, or other clinical licensure.

Why NAV exists

Good coaching should not require a pile of disconnected systems.

Coaches often have to split programming, athlete logs, communication, progress, education, and historical context across different tools. NAV’s product direction is to connect those jobs while preserving the flexibility and human judgment that made the original coaching workflow useful.

Operating principles

Trust comes from keeping the facts straight.

NAV would rather label uncertainty than manufacture precision, and would rather say a feature is in development than advertise it as shipped.

01

Evidence informs the plan.

Claims should match the strength of the evidence and remain separate from coaching interpretation.

02

Context matters.

A useful plan has to fit the person, goal, equipment, schedule, preferences, and training history.

03

Track with a purpose.

Data should answer a real coaching question, not create noise or convert missing information into false certainty.

04

Keep the human in coaching.

Software supports the relationship; it does not replace the coach responsible for judgment.
