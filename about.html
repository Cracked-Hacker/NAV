About NAV

Built at the intersection of coaching and science.

NAV is a developing fitness-coaching, training-software, and exercise-education company founded by Nathan G. Holochwost. Its purpose is to connect thoughtful human coaching with trustworthy training records and useful scientific education. Classroom courses and experimental products remain in development and are not currently available as public offerings; creating an account does not unlock unreleased materials.

Founder · Coach Nate

Meet Coach Nate

Nate graduated from Western Washington University in 2023 with a B.S. in Kinesiology (pre-healthcare specialization), a B.A. in Biology, and a minor in Chemistry. His background includes fitness coaching, clinical support work, laboratory research, anatomy teaching, and a coauthored peer-reviewed publication.

Nate holds a NASM Certified Personal Trainer (CPT) credential. His owner-provided certificate lists May 29, 2025 as its award date and May 29, 2027 as its expiration date.

Why NAV exists

Better tools should support better judgment.

Coaching often spreads programming, workouts, communication, and progress tracking across disconnected tools. NAV aims to make those tasks easier without replacing the coach or turning estimates into facts. The general-science educational work is being developed and reviewed separately from the software.

NAV provides fitness coaching and education—not medical diagnosis, physical therapy, or treatment.
