About NAV

Built to make good training decisions easier to understand and easier to follow.

NAV is an evidence-informed coaching, fitness, performance, and education company. Its public website, Classroom, and NAV Coach app are connected parts of one system with deliberately different jobs.

Coach Nate

Science background. Coaching practice. Clear scope.

Nate Holochwost’s academic background includes biology and kinesiology with chemistry coursework, alongside fitness coaching, personal training certification, and research experience.

NAV uses that background to communicate exercise science carefully—not to imply physician, physical-therapy, dietetics, or other clinical licensure.

Operating principles

  • 01Evidence informs the plan. Claims should match the strength of the evidence.
  • 02Context matters. A good plan has to fit the person, goal, equipment, schedule, and training history.
  • 03Track with a purpose. Data should answer a real question, not create noise.
  • 04Keep the human in coaching. Software supports the relationship; it does not replace it.

NAV ecosystem

Website invites. Classroom teaches. NAV Coach coaches.
